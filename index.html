Dear my Johnee,

There are so many things I want to say, but somehow they’re easier to write than to say out loud. Knowing that you’re leaving and that we might not see each other for a while makes my heart feel heavier than I expected. I’ll miss seeing you, talking to you, and even the little moments that seemed so ordinary but meant so much to me.

Living together wasn’t always easy. We argued more than either of us probably wanted to, but I hope one day u’ll understand that behind every argument was never anger—it was love and concern.

I never wanted to control u or tell u how to live your life. I know u’re your own person, and I respect that. I only wanted u to remember that every choice comes with responsibility, because every decision has consequences.

I love u, and maybe that’s why I worry so much. Sometimes I can already see where things might lead, and I can’t just stand by and watch u get hurt. So even when I raised my voice or said things in a harsh way, it was never because I wanted to hurt u. It was because I was afraid of losing u, or seeing u suffer.

I know my words weren’t always gentle, and maybe they made u feel pressured. I’m sorry for that. I guess I’m just the kind of person who’s tough on the outside but soft on the inside. Everything I said came from a place of love, even if I didn’t always know the best way to express it.

I’ll learn to be more patient and to control my emotions better, so my love doesn’t feel like a burden to u. But I also hope u’ll make me one promise.

Please take responsibility for yourself. Love yourself a little more. Take care of your body, your health, and your future. Don’t neglect yourself, because u’re far more precious than u think.

Thank u for being part of my life. Whether u realize it or not, the time we’ve shared has become something I’ll always treasure. You’ve given me memories that I’ll carry with me wherever I go.

As u start this new chapter, I hope everything goes well for u. Please take good care of yourself. Eat well, get enough rest, and don’t push yourself too hard. No matter where life takes u, I truly hope it brings u happiness, success, and people who treat u with kindness.

Even if distance keeps us apart for a while, I hope this isn’t goodbye forever. I hope one day our paths cross again, and when they do, we’ll smile and say, “It’s been a while.” Until then, I’ll be quietly cheering u on from afar.

Thank u for everything. Take care, stay safe, and don’t forget to smile. I’ll miss u more than these words can ever express.

With all my best wishes,
Someone who cares about u

-Jason Cai
